**Title:** Edith's Last Call is coming to Steam

**Subtitle (event summary):** A short first-person horror game about a welfare check that should have taken ten minutes.

---

On the night of Tuesday, November 9, 1993, the Ashby County Sheriff's Office took a call. A woman's voice gave an address at the end of County Road 9 and said that somebody up there needed company. Then she hung up.

The call came from the Harrow farmhouse. The phone company had cut that line long before.

**Edith's Last Call** puts you in the boots of Deputy Cole Brandt, sent up a forest road alone to knock on Edith Harrow's door and make sure she is all right. Nobody has laid eyes on her in more than a year. Her husband is buried under the willow. Her son never came home from Vietnam. Her daughter stopped visiting. And Edith, who could not stand to be left, found a way to make sure she never would be again.

It is a short game, about 60 to 90 minutes, built to be played in one sitting with the lights off and headphones on. You will search a farmhouse room by room, read what a family left behind, play the home videos still sitting by the TV, and try to find a way out of a house that does not want you to leave.

**What to expect**
- A slow build. The house gets a long time to get under your skin before anything shows itself.
- Something you cannot kill. Your pistol only buys you time.
- A story told through letters, notes, photographs and videotapes from 1969 to 1993.
- A grainy VHS camcorder look (on by default, and you can switch it off).

Add **Edith's Last Call** to your wishlist to hear the moment it is out. If you like it, tell a friend. She would like the company.
